# Changelog

Notable changes to Mob Tweaks are documented here.

## [0.1.1] - Unreleased

### Added

- Ravagers trample fragile blocks while charging and are immune to sweet berry
  bush and cactus damage and wither rose effects. Trampling, plant immunity,
  and turtle-egg crushing have separate settings.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-15

### Added

- Initial 0.1.0 baseline with 20 individually configurable mob tweaks covering
  raids, villagers, shulkers, withers, baby zombie jockeys, slimes, magma cubes,
  ravagers, foxes, vexes, iron golems, horses, skeletons, endermen, and bats.
